[Message 1 — Thu 30 Apr 2026, 11:40 AM]
Dear Students,
As per the Academic Calendar Summer Term 2026, Course Registration for Summer Term 2026 will start from 02 May to 07 May 2026 from 09:00 AM to 11:59 PM.

Important Points:
- Summer Term Calendar can be accessed by CLICKING HERE.
- Course Registration can be done at: erp.iitj.ac.in
- List of courses are AVAILABLE HERE.
- The students may contact their respective Faculty Advisor for approval of the courses.
- UG Students can register for courses during summer up to a maximum of Eight Credits. Summer Term 2026 is primarily for the graduating batch students who have very few backlogs (8 credits or less). For credits above this, students will have to take special approval through proper channels.
- If a student withdraws/drops the course after paying the fee, then the fee will not be refunded.
- For Fee Payment please follow this menu: ERP >> Academic >> Fees.

Fee Details:
| SN | Category of student | Fee per course |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | General/OBC/EWS | Rs 15000/- per course |
| 2 | SC/ST/PH | Rs 2000/- per course |
| 3 | Hostel & Mess Fee | As per the norms of the Office of Hostel Affairs |

No fee will be charged for the courses Design Credit, BTP, MTP, Thesis, RP and Project.

Courses of 100, 200, 300, 400, 500 and/or 600 levels will run if a minimum of 10 students registers for the course. A 700 or 800 level elective course can run with a minimum of 5 registered students. A course not running as a regular course may be offered as a self-study (SS) course subject to approval from higher authorities.
