Dear Students,

This email is applicable only to students who have registered for the course under the Autumn SI for the Academic Year 2026–27.

Please be informed that the Institute follows a zero-tolerance policy towards any form of malpractice or misconduct during examinations.

Before entering the examination hall, you are strongly advised to thoroughly check your belongings and ensure that you are not carrying any unauthorized items (mobile phones, smart watches, incriminating material etc.), whether knowingly or unknowingly. Possession of such items will be treated as a violation, and the matter will be referred to the Disciplinary Committee. This may also adversely affect Pre-Placement, Placement, and Graduation.

As the examination period approaches, kindly take note of the following instructions:
1. All students must be seated in the examination hall at least 15 minutes prior to the scheduled start time.
2. Mobile phones, smartwatches, and all other electronic devices are strictly prohibited inside the examination hall under all circumstances.
3. Strict frisking using metal detectors will be conducted at the examination venue. This procedure will apply to all students without exception, including those arriving late.
4. No extra time will be granted in case of delays caused due to the frisking process.
5. If any prohibited device (such as a mobile phone) is found in your possession, it will be confiscated by security and returned only after completion of due disciplinary procedures by DAC.
6. Students are advised to carefully review the Examination Guidelines for further clarity.
7. Carrying the Institute ID Card is mandatory for all examinations. Entry to the examination venue will not be permitted without a valid ID card.
8. Possession of any unauthorized or incriminating material will invite strict disciplinary action, which may include awarding an 'F' grade in all courses and additional penalties as deemed appropriate by DAC.

You are advised to strictly adhere to these instructions to ensure a smooth examination process and to avoid any inconvenience or disciplinary action.
