Dear Students,

Please refer the Circular No: IITJ/OA/Circular/2022-23/40/2799 dated 27 March 2023 regarding UG Specialization and Minors, & further credit revision in Under Graduate Minor Specialization Circular: IITJ/OA/Circular/2023-24/47/28 dated 11 January 2024, under which UG students can pursue a Specialization or Minor starting from the 5th semester.

Students willing to opt for a Minor or Specialization have to submit the duly signed hard copy of the Minor/Specialization option form (attached) through the authorities (FA, HoD & Specialization Coordinator) by 05:00 pm, 06 August 2026 to the department office, during office hours on any working day. The final list of students selected for specialization or minor will be shared after receiving recommended forms and input from the departments based on the criteria decided by the departments as per senate approved guidelines.

Students currently enrolled in a minor or specialization willing to withdraw from the above need to submit the hard copy of the Withdrawal Form (attached) through the authorities (FA, HoD & Specialization Coordinator) to the department office by 06 August 2026, 5:00 pm.

The detailed guidelines are available on the institute's website. No request for opting or withdrawal will be addressed on email. Please ensure that the credit requirements are met as per the circular. Students are advised to seek proper guidance from their respective Faculty Advisors / DUGC Convener in this regard.

The department office is requested to forward the duly verified application forms (received from students by 05:00 pm, 06 August 2026) to the academic office by 03:00 PM, 07 August 2026 with a forwarding letter. The submitted application form will be verified from the signing authorities. In case of any deviation, the application of the student will be cancelled.

This issues with the concurrence of the competent authority.
In case of any query, prefer to reply and avoid reply all.
