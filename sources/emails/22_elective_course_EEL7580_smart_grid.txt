I request you to kindly circulate the following information regarding the elective course "Introduction to Smart Grid (EEL7580)" among the eligible B.Tech., M.Tech., and Ph.D. students of the Department of Electrical Engineering.

The course provides an introduction to modern smart grid technologies, including renewable energy integration, electric vehicles and charging infrastructure, demand-side management, advanced metering infrastructure, monitoring and protection, and emerging trends in power systems. Along with the theoretical concepts, the course will incorporate examples and case studies from real-world power systems and industrial applications to help students understand how these technologies are implemented in practice.

The course also includes a short, application-oriented project that exposes students to real-world smart grid challenges, industry practices, and emerging research problems, enhancing their practical understanding of the subject.

Interested students may register for the course during the course registration period as per the institute's academic regulations.
