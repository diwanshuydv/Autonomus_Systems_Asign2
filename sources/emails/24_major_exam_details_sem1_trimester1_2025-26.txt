Dear All (Except B.Sc. AI students)

The following Major Examinations are scheduled as per the details mentioned below:
- Major Examinations of Semester-1 of Academic Year 2025-26 from 20 to 26 November 2025 as per the Semester Academic Calendar for AY 2025-26
- Major Examinations of Trimester-1 of Academic Year 2025-26 from 28 to 30 November 2025 as per the Trimester Academic Calendar for AY 2025-26

In this regard, please find below the details for the same:
1. Offline Student Mapping
2. Online Student Mapping
3. Examination Venue
4. Examination Guidelines

Important Points:
- Students should refer to the above details to verify their name and roll number. In case of any discrepancy, students may either visit the Examination Section at the Office of Academic Affairs on working days or email examhelp@iitj.ac.in. The Examination Venue and other details will be shared after 12 November 2025.
- In case of Examination Overload/Clash, students will discuss the same with any one Course Instructor (preferably the one with less class strength). The concerned Course Instructors are requested to fill in the Examination Shifting details in the GOOGLE SHEET on or before 10 November 2025 and suggest another date/time slot within the timetable that is suitable for all registered students.
- Please go through the Examination Guidelines thoroughly to avoid any confusion.
- Executive/Online Students will receive the examination link a few days before the examination date.
- Bringing/Carrying the Institute ID Card during all the examinations is mandatory.
- In case of missing the examinations, please refer to Page 8 of Examination Guidelines, fill the application form available at Page No. 10, and submit it to the Parent Department before 30 November 2025. Department Offices are requested to submit duly signed and verified application forms to the Office of Academic Affairs by 02 December 2025.

In case of any query, prefer to reply and avoid replying all.
