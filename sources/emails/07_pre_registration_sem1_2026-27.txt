Important Points:-
- Regular Students are not allowed to Register in Executive Slots and Executive Students are not allowed to Register in Regular Slots.
- Regular and Executive students can register for courses floated in hybrid slots (Slots like O, P, Q, R, S).
- Courses are to be registered slot-wise, and students will be allowed to register for only one course per slot.
- Students with backlogs are advised to register for the Basic Courses and complete them before registering for the Department Compulsory Courses. In case of confusion, please contact the Faculty Advisor.
- The students must register for the appropriate non-grade courses. It is mandatory to complete the non-graded courses for the degree requirements.
- For any course-related query (course type, credit requirements, etc.), kindly discuss with your Faculty Advisor.

8. If you find any discrepancy in course type or course category, kindly contact your departmental timetable committee member.
9. For Ph.D. students, refer to Circular No. IITJ/OAA/Circular/2024-25/5587 – "Guidelines for submission of thesis and subsequent registration to the next semester for PhD students".
10. For Institute Humanities electives, the upper cap on the number of students is 80 (allocation based on CGPA earned till the last semester).
11. For Minor in Entrepreneurship and Department Specialization in Engineering Innovation, a scrutiny committee will be formed for selecting the students. Upper cap for Minor in Management courses is 40.
12. For pre-registration, there will be no upper cap on elective courses; an upper cap will be implemented during main registration. First preference will be given to students enrolled in the course in a category other than open elective.
