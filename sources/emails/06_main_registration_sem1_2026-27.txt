Important Points:-
- Regular Students are not allowed to Register in Executive Slots and Executive Students are not allowed to Register in Regular Slots.
- Regular and Executive students can register for courses floated in hybrid slots (Slots Like O, P, Q, R, S).
- Courses are to be registered slot-wise, and students will be allowed to register for only one course per slot.
- Students with backlogs are advised to register for the Basic Courses and complete them before registering for the Department Compulsory Courses. In case you have any confusion, please consult the Faculty Advisor.
- The students must register for the appropriate non-grade courses. It is mandatory to complete the non-graded courses for the degree requirements.
- For any course-related query (course type, credit requirements, etc.), kindly discuss with your faculty advisor. Faculty advisors also have the option to register courses, but only within the deadline.

"Limit of credits to check course level = earned credits by the student till the last semester + currently registered credits in the current semester."
a) This assumes that the student will pass all the courses. If there is a shortfall, the students will be dropped from the courses during the main registration.
b) Such students will be permitted to register for other courses during the main registration.
