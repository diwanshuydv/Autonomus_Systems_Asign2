Title: Strict Warning: Damage to Hostel Property Will Not Be Tolerated
Category: Rules & Regulations / Hostel Discipline
Source: IIT Jodhpur institute email (adha@iitj.ac.in — Associate Dean, Hostel Affairs)
Date: 14 Sep 2026; final warning 26 Sep 2026; follow-up 19 Oct 2026
Recipient: All Hostel Residents (allhostels@iitj.ac.in)
----------------------------------------------------------------

[Message 1 — Mon 14 Sep 2026, 5:16 PM]
Dear Students,
It has come to the notice of the Office of Hostel Affairs that hostel property and facilities are being damaged by some students. Such acts of carelessness, indiscipline, and vandalism are completely unacceptable and will not be tolerated.
The hostel facilities are provided for the benefit and comfort of all students. Damaging institute property not only causes financial loss to the Institute but also adversely affects the living environment and the facilities available to fellow residents.
This email serves as a strict warning to all hostel residents.
1. All students are hereby directed to refrain from any activity that may cause damage to hostel property, including furniture, doors, electrical fittings, washroom facilities, common areas, and other institute assets.
2. Any student found directly involved in damaging hostel property shall be held accountable and subjected to strict disciplinary action as per the applicable Institute rules and regulations.
3. Students found indirectly involved, including those assisting, encouraging, facilitating, or concealing such acts, shall also be held accountable and may face strict disciplinary action.
4. If such incidents continue, the Institute will take stringent action against the students found responsible. No leniency will be shown in cases of deliberate damage or misconduct.
5. Students are expected to maintain discipline, respect institute property, and report any such incidents to the concerned Hostel Caretaker/Warden or the Office of Hostel Affairs.
Let it be clearly understood that hostel accommodation is a facility provided by the Institute and not a license to misuse or damage institute property. Every student is responsible for maintaining the hostel's dignity, cleanliness, safety, and discipline.

[Message 2 — Sat 26 Sep 2026, 11:53 PM — Final and strict warning]
Despite the earlier communication, incidents of damage and misuse of hostel property and facilities continue to be reported. This email may be treated as a final and strict warning to all hostel residents.
All students are hereby directed to ensure that no hostel property or facility is damaged, misused, tampered with, or deliberately defaced. This includes, but is not limited to, doors, fire extinguishers, furniture, electrical fittings, lights, fans, washroom facilities, walls, common areas, and other Institute assets.
1. Any student found directly responsible for damaging hostel property will be held accountable and subjected to strict disciplinary action as per the applicable Institute rules and regulations.
2. Students who assist, encourage, facilitate, participate in, or conceal such acts will also be held accountable and may face disciplinary action.
3. Where the person responsible for the damage cannot be immediately identified, the matter may be investigated through available information, including CCTV footage, reports from hostel staff, and other relevant evidence.
4. Any further incident of deliberate damage or vandalism will be viewed very seriously. No leniency will be shown in such cases.
5. Students are required to immediately report any incident of damage or misuse of hostel property to the concerned Warden/Caretaker or the Office of Hostel Affairs.

[Message 3 — Mon 19 Oct 2026, 4:46 PM]
It has been observed that, despite repeated strict warnings, some students continue to engage in unethical activities and damage institute property. Such misconduct is unacceptable and will be dealt with seriously. All students are urged to refrain from such activities, as these actions may lead to disciplinary action, including financial penalties.
