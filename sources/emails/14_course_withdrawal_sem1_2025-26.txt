Dear Students (Except UG First Year & Diploma),

As per the Academic Calendar of AY 2025-26 Semester-1, the last date of Course Withdrawal is 15 October 2025.

Kindly follow the process mentioned below for the same:
- The application form is attached to this email and will be accepted in hard copy only.
- Students are required to (i) fill out the Application Form (copy enclosed), (ii) get the necessary signatures of the Course Instructor(s) and Faculty Advisor.
- This Application form must be submitted to the Student's Parent Department office by 15 October 2025, for further submission to the Office of Academic Affairs. Department offices are requested to kindly check and submit the duly signed forms to the Office of Academic Affairs by 16 October 2025.
- Executive students can submit the approved Application form to Registration IIT Jodhpur (registration@iitj.ac.in) after getting necessary signatures/e-signature/email approval of the Course Instructor(s) and Faculty Advisor, with a marking copy to the Instructor and Faculty Advisor.

Note- For any query, please write to Registration IIT Jodhpur and prefer reply instead of reply all.

With regards,
Ajay, Registration Cell, Office of Academic Affairs, IIT Jodhpur
