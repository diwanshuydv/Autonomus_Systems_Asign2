(B) Important Safety Advisory: Prohibition of Candles and Open Flames in Hostel Rooms
From: Associate Dean, Hostel Affairs (ADHA) <adha@iitj.ac.in>
To: All Hostel Residents <allhostels@iitj.ac.in>
Date: Sun, 27 Sep 2026, 12:18 AM

Dear Students,
You are advised to exercise utmost caution and adhere to the following fire safety instructions:
1. Do not light candles, diyas, incense sticks, or any other open flame inside hostel rooms.
2. Under no circumstances should any burning candle or open flame be placed near curtains, clothes, towels, bedsheets, furniture, paper, or any other combustible material.
3. Students must not engage in any activity that may create a fire hazard or compromise the safety of other hostel residents.
4. In case of any fire or emergency, immediately inform the Hostel Caretaker/Warden and Security personnel and follow the prescribed emergency procedures.
5. Students are also advised to familiarize themselves with the location and proper use of the fire extinguishers installed in their respective hostels.

A minor incident can escalate into a major fire within seconds and may result in serious injury, loss of life, and extensive damage to Institute property. The safety of every hostel resident is a collective responsibility.

All students are therefore advised to take this matter seriously and strictly comply with hostel safety norms. Any violation of safety instructions that puts students, staff, or Institute property at risk may invite appropriate action as per Institute rules.
