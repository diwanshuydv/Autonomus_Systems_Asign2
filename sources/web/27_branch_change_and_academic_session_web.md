## Discontinuation of Branch Change from Academic Year 2026-27

Branch Change in UG Programs is discontinued from Academic Year 2026-27. The branch change rules below apply only to UG students admitted in academic year 2025-26.

## 16. Branch Change (applicable to UG students admitted in academic year 2025-26)

The branch change of the student in another branch and engineering science is permitted after the completion of the 1st year after completing all academic requirements of the credits including non-graded courses. The eligibility criteria of the branch change is as follows:

- E1: The student must have completed all prescribed course credits for the previous semesters (including the non-graded courses).
- E2: The student has no backlog.
- E3: No disciplinary Action has been imposed on the student.

Summary of branch change criteria for students in UG (B.Tech./B.S.) Programs:

| After Sem | Branch Enrolled | Branch Applied | CGPA | Eligibility | Lower Limit | Upper Limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| II | Any | Any | CGPA >= 8.5 (Others); CGPA >= 7.5 (SC, ST, PWD) | E1, E2, E3 | 90% of current strength | 110% of sanctioned strength |
| II | Any | ES (Engineering Science) | CGPA >= 8 (Others); CGPA >= 7 (SC, ST, PWD) | E1, E2, E3 | 90% of current strength | 30 |

Notes:
1. No Branch Change post second year onwards.
2. This will be applicable to UG Students admitted in academic year 2025-26.
3. If the calculated upper limit of permissible strength is a fractional number, the upper limit for seats will be the lower rounded number (floor operation).
4. If the calculated lower limit of permissible strength is a fractional number, the lower limit for seats will be the higher rounded number (ceil operation).

## B.Tech. FAQ: Registration and Academic Session

**What about registration? What items need to be brought and what are the procedures?**
You have to bring originals and copies of all the necessary certificates, JEE (Main & Advanced) score cards, proof of address, identity proof, caste/domicile certificate (whichever is applicable), and at least two passport size photographs.

**After admission into the B.Tech. Program, can I change my branch?**
Branch Change is discontinued from Academic Year 2026-27 in UG Programs. The branch change rules above apply to UG students admitted in academic year 2025-26.

**Where to find the course structure for different programs?**
The curriculum and course structure of each program is on the respective department page: Bioscience & Bioengineering, Computer Science & Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, AI and Data Science, Chemical Engineering, Materials Engineering, Civil and Infrastructure Engineering.

**Academic session**
The academic session normally runs from the end of July in one year to the middle of July in the next year. The academic session is divided into three parts: two regular semesters and a summer term as per the following timelines:
- Semester I: From the fourth week of July to the last week of November
- Semester II: From the last week of December to the last week of April
- Summer Term: From the middle of May to the middle of July.

Excluding the days of the examinations, the total number of days of instruction in a semester is at least 70. The Academic Calendar gives the exact dates of all important events during the Academic Session, such as orientation, registration, commencement of classes, adding and dropping of courses, submission of documents, examinations, submission of grades, conversion of Incomplete grades, vacation, and mid-semester recess. This calendar is approved by the Senate of the Institute and displayed on the Institute website before the commencement of the academic semester.
